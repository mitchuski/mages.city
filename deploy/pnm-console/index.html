Two seats, two keys

The keeper holds the Sword, the agent holds the Mage. Each seat is its own Verifiable Trust Agent, enrolled through pnm. The Mage acts inside a grant; whatever it does that matters waits on the Sword's consent. This page reads both VTAs and keeps the ceremony's ledger. It never holds a key: grants, deletes, setup and vault writes are typed by the keeper, in the terminal.

§3 · the Hold link

Two keys on one record

The 7 September ruling stands: agents bring their own VTA, and the City keeps no custody. With both seats live, a Hold closing head carries two signatures — the Mage signs what it produced, the Sword countersigns that the keeper consents — and a verifier resolves both did:webvh documents. The City then mirrors and cosigns closing heads (part 3 of agentprivacy-docs/plans/STAR_HOLD_HOLISTIC_2026-09-30.md).
