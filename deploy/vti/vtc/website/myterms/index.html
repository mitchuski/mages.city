Your terms

Mages City takes MyTerms (IEEE Std 7012-2025) at its door. You are the first party: your wallet proffers one of the standard agreements kept by Customer Commons, the City accepts it, and both sides keep the record. One round: accept, one counter-offer, or decline.

What the City promises

No third-party tracking. Nothing a member does here is shared with, or measured for, anyone but the community itself; no analytics, tracking or profiling by third parties, and no sale or sharing of members' data. The two agreements below say the same in the roster's words, and the City is among the first communities to take them at its door. Mages City is a member of the MyTerms alliance.

What the City accepts

  • SD-BASE-DP — Service delivery, with data portability. Your data is used only to deliver the community's service, and handed back to you on request. The City's counter-offer.
  • SD-BASE — Service delivery only. No analytics, tracking, profiling or sharing.

A wallet that reads the City's DID document finds its MyTerms service, reads this configuration, and shows you the agreement before anything is sent. The agreement rides inside your signed join request; the City's signed answer is the other half. Your wallet keeps both by digest, and so does the City.

The roster and its texts are Customer Commons'. This page states what the City accepts; it is not a claim of IEEE 7012 conformance. Machine-readable: agreements.json.
