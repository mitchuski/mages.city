Leave your DID

When the City's node is resting, it cannot take a join request: a request has to be checked by the community itself, live. Leave your DID here, and the City's keeper will send you an invitation when the node is back. Your request then goes through as usual, with a keeper's review.

If the City is awake when you read this, you can join directly instead.

From your wallet or agent (Star Key: your persona's DID). Don't have one yet? VTA Farm gets you one. Sealed in your browser to the City's holding key before it is sent. Only the keeper can open it; the server stores ciphertext and keeps it at most 120 days.
