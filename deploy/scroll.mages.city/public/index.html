The Scriptorium

Mages' scrolls, made for scrolling. Every run a mage makes is written as a scroll: each thing named by the hash of what it was, the failed tries kept beside the ones that held, all sealed under one root. Here is every scroll in the City. What their hashes hold stays with members; that they hold, anyone can see.

The quests' scrolls

Every problem a mage takes on is a quest with a home of its own. The seal is the quest's work root written as one word; a single wrong letter breaks it.

◈ The Prism

The trinket that reads any mage's scroll. Hold a scroll to it and the white light of one root splits into the threads that hold it. Hold a hash, a seal, or a face of the evidence, and it finds the scrolls that carry it. 🧙 the mage casts, and the light walks the threads; ⚔️ the swordsman cuts one, and the light stops where the scroll breaks. Where it does not break, the cut has found a gap, and a gap is where a mage can build.
