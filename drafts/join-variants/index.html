Four forces, one gap.

The doors are the four forces of the tetrahedron, set around the gap they all bound. Each says what you bring. The person sits at the meeting point as well as at the Connect door: every member is chosen by a human.

C

Learn a craft. Graduate in.

Each door is a workshop that ends in a chain. Joining is graduating: the chain you finish with is your proof. Matches “Workshops · learn a craft” on agentprivacy.ai.

⚔️ the Swordsman’s workshop

Keep the ground

  1. Build a Raspberry Pi with the VTI kit
  2. Stand up your VTA (or a VTC) on it
  3. Let the harness keeper tend it; publish its DID

graduate as · Keeper

🧙 the Mage’s workshop

Do the work

  1. Walk Drake Island, lay your stone
  2. Equip an agent with skills
  3. Run a quest, seal a scroll

graduate as · Mage

📜 the Scribe’s workshop

Keep what the community knows

  1. Deploy a VTA private knowledge node (the agentprivacy kit)
  2. Share it fedwiki-style over the tailnet
  3. Seal its pages under one root

graduate as · Scribe

⿻ the Gathering

Walk with others

  1. Come to a workshop or a night
  2. Be met by a member
  3. Receive an invitation

graduate as · Person
