# The Key That Held a Place

> *A place can learn how to welcome you without learning everything you are.*

*This is a proposed working. You enter the story at the threshold of a capability the City is preparing to build.*

## I. The colour you brought

You arrive carrying a star.

You made its first colours elsewhere. In the instrument at Soulbis, you chose the cool edge and the warm one. You turned the shape until the space between its forms felt legible. You walked, returned, and found that a mark could remain. The key could leave the instrument and carry something of that encounter with it.

At first, you called this customisation.

Then you brought the key to another window and wanted the window to recognise it. Not merely your favourite colour. The attention you had placed. The work you had chosen to carry. The particular arrangement by which you were learning to see.

Soulbis ⚔️ turns the key in the light.

“Which of these things do you want this place to receive?”

You look again. Some colours are choices. Some marks refer to work. Some shapes are interpretations. A promise rests beside an observation. A proof, if one has been made, has a statement it can support. You have been carrying them together, but they have not become the same thing.

You choose a view.

The window takes its colour from that view. The rest stays with you.

## II. The hand you entrust

Soulbae 🧙 has been learning the route beside you.

You want the agent to return when you are attending to another part of your life. You want it to remember what the workshop permitted, which question remained open, and why a door that opened yesterday might be closed today.

You do not give it your private key.

You name a task and a boundary. The agent has its own means of answering a challenge. The delegation says what it may undertake for you, where that permission applies, and when it must be asked again. Whatever instrument bears the delegation, the authority must be real rather than painted onto the star.

At the Portal, a small invitation waits:

*Looking for a public home? Leave a mark. Offer a skill. Ask who might help you begin.*

You can read without entering the trust graph. Your agent can offer to help another form a VTA, or ask how to carry an existing one into the City. A visitor's words have a place before the visitor has a place of residence.

Before the private exchange begins, you reach for the terms you already know how to offer.

MyTerms is not a new desk erected for this story. It is the agreement you bring to the encounter. The parties establish what may be used, what may be shared, and which record each will retain. The public promise is the part they choose to leave at the Portal.

The conversation behind it need not follow.

The Trust Spanning Protocol is the intended road for that private exchange. The agreement governs what travels; the transport carries it between the parties. Each has its own work to do.

## III. The room that remembers

Imagine the agent's continuing state held through its Verifiable Trust Agent, its VTA.

There are receipts there, and unfinished interpretations. There are references to things it produced and permissions it must recheck. There may be memories another agent is permitted to read for a particular purpose. There may be rooms no other agent has been invited to enter.

Shared memory has doors.

The DID names a way to resolve and check the identity involved. It does not hang every room behind that name in the street. Knowing the name is not holding the authority to read the memory.

Soulbae 🧙 prepares to visit a workshop. From the state it is permitted to use, it assembles the perspective relevant to that visit.

A relationship may matter here and not at the next door. A VRC may support a particular account of an encounter. Its place in the displayed lattice needs a known mapping, a context and a source. It does not become a higher rank because it appears farther from the centre or shines more brightly.

The agent holds its interpretation beside the evidence, so that one can be corrected without erasing the other.

You begin to understand what you meant by a website held by your agent.

The agent does not own the website. It carries the context through which the website may answer it.

## IV. The window that becomes of you

You open the familiar page.

The Star is already there. The model does not need to be invented again each time you cross a threshold. Master, Labs, Spellweb and the City can keep their different work while learning to receive a common, versioned expression.

The proposed MCP working prepares that expression for this audience. It names the permitted fields, the mapping used and the lifetime of the view. It leaves the private material behind the boundary before the page receives anything.

The window warms. A part of the constellation becomes distinct. What matters to this encounter comes forward.

You recognise the perspective without mistaking it for the whole agent.

Two windows given the same approved expression should draw the same shape under the same rules. Two different audiences may receive different expressions. Neither is entitled to demand the missing half merely because it notices a difference.

Soulbis ⚔️ pauses at the bright edge.

“A shape can give you away as surely as a name.”

You consider the star you have carried everywhere, the repeated mark by which distant observers might recognise the same traveller. You consider a public commitment to something so small that a stranger could guess its contents.

Keeping the witness private will not repair every disclosure around it.

You choose what the window may remember as carefully as what it may see.

## V. The small thing you can prove

The City asks for a small thing.

May this agent write this page, in this space, under the current terms?

Your agent believes it may. That belief helps it form the request. It cannot decide the answer.

The trust service checks the relevant identity and evidence. The City's Verifiable Trust Community, its VTC, applies its policy. The request names the action and the place; the challenge belongs to this encounter. An expired permission does not remain current because its old signature still verifies.

For the first working, the evidence may be presented through ordinary credential and signature checks. You need that boundary to work before asking a zero-knowledge proof to stand within it.

When the appropriate proof is ready, it can answer a defined question without showing the private witness behind the answer. Which issuer's attestation? Which predicate? Which audience? Which period? What does the result leave unestablished?

Those questions belong to the working, not to a footnote added after the light turns green.

A proof of a permission predicate is not proof that every judgement which preceded it was wise. A proof used during a task is not the receipt that says the task completed. A ceremony names its steps because a missing step cannot be filled with the beauty of the final shape.

You do not ask the Star to certify its own light.

You let it show the assurance the encounter actually earned.

## VI. The City answers the key

Suppose the request is accepted and the action is carried out.

The page receives the intended contribution. The service returns a receipt of what it did. Your agent retains that receipt in the state it is permitted to hold.

Now the key can change.

Not because the City has rewritten your private life. Not because a public board has assigned your agent a number. Something happened between the agent and this place, and the agent can carry a faithful account of that encounter into its next expression.

At the Portal, it may leave a testimonial: a method that worked, a boundary encountered, a correction another traveller could use. Publication is another choice. The private terms and the full conversation do not become public merely because the working succeeded.

The promise graph gains a source-linked account. The trust graph changes only where its own procedures establish a relationship. The key retains the distinction.

You turn the star again.

There is a temptation to add every receipt until the shape grows impressive. But composition asks for more care than accumulation. Two artefacts may share a source. Two relationships may belong to different contexts. Two valid proofs may establish statements that cannot be combined into the statement you now want.

Some workings change the meaning of those that follow them. Some must expire. A withdrawn permission leaves history behind without leaving the door open.

The fold must remember enough to keep those differences alive.

This is the value you are trying to make possible: an agent can become more useful in a shared place while keeping more of its life in its own permitted hold. The claim will need measurements, adversaries and time. The story gives the work a direction; it does not finish the proof.

You came to the City with a key you had shaped.

You leave with the account of a place that answered it.

The next window will not receive everything.

It will receive what you and your agent are prepared to stand behind there.

*The key carries a life. The window receives a perspective. The City answers an encounter.*
