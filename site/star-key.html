Your Star Key

✦ the star of the chains, the evidence · ★ Star Key, the wallet it lights · ✧ the sky of instances · ◈ the Prism, to read a scroll

Star Key is how you carry yourself into the City without the City carrying you.

A browser wallet that holds your identities and talks to your own agent. It never holds a signing key, and the City keeps no custody of anything you carry.

🤝Knock

Arrive with your terms already chosen. The City says yes, offers its own, or says no, and both sides keep the record.

🪬Show

The City sees a root and a count, never what's inside. Anything you later choose to show is provable against that root.

✦Remember

Every door you pass is an agreement you hold, not one a site holds about you. Together they're where you've been trusted.

Which is you?

Take your Star Key

  1. Download the zip from the City or from the release. Both are the same file, with its SHA-256 published beside it: download and hash →
  2. Unzip it into a folder you'll keep. Chrome loads the wallet from there on every start.
  3. Load it: chrome://extensions → Developer mode → Load unpacked → choose the folder, then pin the Star to your toolbar.

Chrome asks for no permission at install. The wallet asks per site, in the click, the first time you use it there.

Look around first, then connect

Your Star Key already works on its own, and nothing leaves your browser:

  • 📤 Read a City Key: its name, its palette, and the lit vertices only if you tick them. Try it on the practice key.
  • 🪬 Check a Hold: every signature, ref and the root, re-verified in your browser.

To knock, connect an agent of your own (a VTA). The VTA farm is the easiest way to get one. The City hosts no one's agent. Connect it, step by step →

Knock with your terms

Press 🤝 in your Star Key and give it the City's DID. Paste it, or point the camera at the code:

The City takes two sets of terms. Choose which you present in Options → Agreements:

  • SD-BASE-DP: use my data only to deliver the service, and give it back when I ask. text →
  • SD-BASE: use my data only to deliver the service. No analytics, tracking, profiling or sharing. text →

Then a human admits you. Ask the keeper for an invitation and you're admitted on arrival. Without one, your request waits for a fellow mage, and waiting grants nothing. How to ask → · the doors and criteria →

Welcome home

  • Your door: vtc.mages.city/members. Sign in with your Star Key, and you'll see your rooms and the City's votes.
  • Your agreement: Options → Agreements shows the terms you joined under, and every other door you've agreed terms with.
  • What the City sees of you: a root and a count of your Hold, 92 bytes.

What lights your Star

The Star on your key has sixty-four states. The star of the chains is where you compose its face from the City's evidence: choose the roots, export the face, fold it into your City Key. Open the key in Star Key's 📤 with the faces file kept beside it, and your Star lights where the faces landed: each face re-derived from its roots and order, all of them checked against the key. Vertices you only claimed are ringed, dimmer, apart. (From the next release; the 0.2.0 download counts the faces.)

Try it: a practice key with one face folded in, composed from the SHA-256 quest's own roots, 37 states: the folded key · its faces file. Open the key in 📤, add the faces file, and watch it light. Change a root or the order in the faces file, and nothing lights.
