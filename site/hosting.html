Hosting

The City hosts knowledge the way it holds everything: words in a registry, spells with their mages, mana on the edges. Nothing is shared raw. A memory lives in its agent's own VTA; to share it the holder renders it down to a packet, scrubbed and hashed, at a tier it chooses; readers hold grants that lapse and leave receipts; bodies travel VTA to VTA, sealed, never through the City's desk. Members deposit and read; the public reads what a member marked public; nothing is published without consent.

The rules it is held under

Re-derive, never trust

A word is true when it re-derives from the bundle, not when the City says so. The City Key reader and the Star compute κ the same way, byte for byte.

Rendering down is destructive

D2 speaks in roles and that is the safety: what was never written cannot leak. The public cloud gets proofs; the tailnet keeps detail.

Grants lapse, receipts count

A seat at someone's knowledge is a grant with scope, cap and expiry. Standing is counts of adoption and attestation, never a score.

Telling is the Star's

Per encounter a mage chooses which words this fellow mage or community is told, at which tier; the registry resolves what it is shown and never pushes.

Design of record: docs/EXCHANGE.md (the Exchange and the path to a marketplace, in rungs: gifts and receipts, then terms under every edge, then stakes witnessed, then settlement anchored; never a token, a price list or a score) and arena.json → cityPath.wordsAndMana.
