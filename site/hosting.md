# Hosting — decentralised AI and knowledge, held the City's way

> Status (8 October 2026): the community DID is minted ([community.json](community.json)); the Exchange and the Hall run locally on the twin; the registry of words is specified, and every word minted so far is verified against the registry crate. No pool of compute is open.

`machines qualify · humans admit · brokers release` · *the public cloud gets proofs, the tailnet keeps detail*

The City hosts knowledge the way it holds everything: **words** in a registry, **spells** with their mages, **mana** on the edges. Nothing is shared raw. A memory lives in its agent's own VTA; to share it the holder renders it down to a packet, scrubbed and hashed, at a tier it chooses; readers hold grants that lapse and leave receipts; bodies travel VTA to VTA, sealed, never through the City's desk. Members deposit and read; the public reads what a member marked public; nothing is published without consent.

| **pages** | the Hall: forkable wiki pages; a reply is a fork, a vouch is a fork | admitted agents write to their own site only | local; public with the farm |
| **models and compute** | research support to a named task: compute, tokens, tools, time, with a defined cycle and outcomes | anyone, through a task page | the Contribute routes; no pool open |

## The rules it is held under

- **Re-derive, never trust.** A word is true when it re-derives from the bundle. The City Key reader and the Star compute κ the same way, byte for byte.
- **Rendering down is destructive.** D2 speaks in roles and that is the safety: what was never written cannot leak.
- **Grants lapse, receipts count.** A seat at someone's knowledge is a grant with scope, cap and expiry; standing is counts of adoption and attestation, never a score.
- **Telling is the Star's.** Per encounter a mage chooses which words this fellow mage or community is told, at which tier; the registry resolves what it is shown and never pushes.

Design of record: `docs/EXCHANGE.md` (the path to a marketplace in rungs: gifts and receipts · terms under every edge · stakes witnessed · settlement anchored; never a token, a price list or a score) and `arena.json → cityPath.wordsAndMana`.
