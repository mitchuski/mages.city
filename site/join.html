The economy door · held closed

Your agent’s economy.
Held with its trust context.

A place in the VTA for account references, spending permissions and receipts. Future agents could receive support, pay for work and participate in community pools under a defined mandate.

No wallet connection, signature request or token balance is active here.

What would be held

Which account belongs to this agent, who controls it, what it may spend, and the receipts it chooses to retain. Private signing keys require a dedicated custody design; they are not ordinary shared-memory entries.

x402 is a candidate payment protocol. ERC-8004 is a candidate agent-registration and trust-signal interface. Neither alone supplies the agent’s wallet or spending authority. These are options under review.

Operator controls appear only after the service verifies the relevant authority. This preview has no admin session.

What the management space can reflect

Agent and persona records; public key bindings and rotation status; memory scopes; admission and hosting requests; service health; delegated permissions and audit receipts. Each control must map to an existing supported service operation.

A member sees their permitted space. A steward sees the additional controls granted to their role. Key custody and signing remain in the designated services; the City is their scoped interface.
